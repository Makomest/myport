Gameplay

  • Place a bet - step into the arena.
  • Automated fight. Win - an item drops (spins on the loot reel) and raises your multiplier. Loss - bust, the bet is gone.
  • CASH OUT to bank bet × multiplier, or CONTINUE to push further. The multiplier grows with no ceiling; the payout is capped by the max-win limit.

Loot system (items)

Every win drops an item of one rarity (6 tiers: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic + jackpots), revealed on the loot reel.

  • 3 item slots. A new tier fills a free slot; once all are taken, a rarer item replaces the weakest.
  • Stacking is the key to big wins. A duplicate of an equipped tier stacks (Mythic x2, x3 ...) for the full tier multiplier; total = tier^count across slots - the multiplier grows exponentially with no ceiling.
  • A weaker drop = consolation: a minimal bump (+2%) and a chance at a star - a win is never empty.

Promo & retention - Daily Arena (star tournaments)

The star leaderboard turns solo play into a multiplayer competition every day. Players earn stars for wins (shown on the loot reel; they do not affect RTP or payouts). A real cash prize every day directly lifts LTV.

  • Account level boosts the star chance - easier to break into the top.
  • Levels and the leaderboard reset every 24 hours (UTC) - everyone starts at level 1, a fresh fair race, the daily-return habit.
  • Pool, prize split and schedule are configurable per campaign.

Math and fairness

The house edge is taken once on entry, every CONTINUE is EV-neutral → RTP is invariant to player strategy. Validated by Monte-Carlo (millions of runs), with a machine-readable PAR sheet for the lab (GLI-19 / iTech Labs). Each outcome is derived from serverSeed + clientSeed + nonce (HMAC-SHA256); the hash is published before the round and the seed revealed after - with a built-in verifier.

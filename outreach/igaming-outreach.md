# RoniGaming — iGaming outreach pack (EN)

Goal: get studios, providers and aggregators to look at the games and open a conversation
(supply / integration / partnership), plus job applications to HR contacts.

**Sender:** Roni — iGaming game designer & developer
**Portfolio + playable demos:** https://ronigames.org
**Email:** ronigames.hq@gmail.com · **Telegram:** @Roneviy

**The games (used in every email):**
- **Gladiator** — crash × slot hybrid. Provably fair, 96% RTP, daily tournament. Loot gear, stack multipliers, cash out or risk it.
- **Redline Rush** — real-time multiplayer crash racing. Provably fair, 97% RTP, ×10,000 max win, HTML5 / PWA.
- Both are **live and playable in the browser, no signup**.

**Sending rules**
- Send **individually** (one recipient per email). Never CC/BCC the whole list — it kills deliverability and looks like spam.
- Keep the subject line; it's written to survive a busy inbox.
- Swap `{Company}` and paste the per-company subject + opening line, then the shared body.
- Send 10–20/day, not all at once, so you don't trip spam filters.
- Attach nothing on the first email — the link does the work. Send game sheets only when they reply.

From code to a live slot

Every game is built in the open here. Move through the steps and the machine assembles itself, from a math model into a board you can play.

Step 01

Math model and code

Every slot starts as numbers. The paytable, hit rate, volatility, and RTP are modeled and simulated millions of times before a single pixel is drawn.

Step 02

The reels take shape

Reel count, rows, and ways to win. This grid is the skeleton the entire game hangs on, so it is set before anything else.

Step 03

Symbols on the board

Low pays, high pays, wilds, and scatters. Each symbol is placed and weighted so the board stays readable at a single glance.

Step 04

Bonus and win logic

Paylines, free spins, and multipliers. These are the moments that make a player lean in and keep coming back.

Step 05

UI and launch

Controls, balance, autoplay, and a responsive build. The game is integrated to your platform and prepared for certification.
